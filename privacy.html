Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-20

This Privacy Policy describes how Snipzoning ("the Service") handles information in connection with its use of TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram APIs to publish short-form video content.

1. Who this applies to

The Service is operated for a small number of social media accounts owned by the Service operator. It does not offer sign-up, accounts, or any user-facing product to the general public.

2. Information we access

The Service accesses OAuth access/refresh tokens issued directly by each connected platform (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram) after the account owner explicitly authorizes access through that platform's own consent screen. These tokens are used solely to publish video content to that same account and are stored only on infrastructure controlled by the operator.

The Service does not collect, sell, or share personal data belonging to viewers of the published content, and does not use any platform data for advertising or profiling purposes.

3. Data retention

Authorization tokens are retained only for as long as needed to operate the Service and are deleted if the connected account is disconnected.

4. Third-party platforms

Use of TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram through the Service is additionally governed by each platform's own privacy policy and developer terms.

5. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to snipzoning@outlook.com.
